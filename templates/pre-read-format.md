# Pre-read Format

Use this format for documents sent before a meeting for pre-reading, silent reading and live discussion.
Follows the Minto pyramid: recommendation first, then options, then evidence.

---

## Template

```
# [Project/Topic] - Pre-read

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**For:** [Names or roles of reviewers]
**Decision needed:** [What we need to leave with, one sentence]

---

## Recommendation

[State the recommendation directly. 2-3 sentences max. No build-up, no context-setting, lead with the answer.]

## Options considered

**Option A: [Name]** *(recommended)*
[What it is and why it's the right call.]

**Option B: [Name]**
[What it is and why we're not choosing it.]

**Option C: [Name]** *(if applicable)*
[What it is and why we're not choosing it.]

## Supporting context

[Analysis, data, user research, or rationale that backs the recommendation. Readers who trust the recommendation may skip this. Those who need convincing will read it.]

---

## Topics for live discussion

*These require debate or judgment and cannot be resolved async.*

- [Open question or tension that needs the group]
- [Risk or tradeoff worth surfacing]

## Questions that can be answered async

*Leave a comment, I'll respond before or after the meeting.*

- [Anticipated question]
- [Anticipated question]
```

---

## Rules

- **Lead with the recommendation, always.** Never open with background or process. The reader should know the answer within 10 seconds.
- **Options section is mandatory.** Shows you considered alternatives. Minimum 2 options. If there's truly only one option, state why others were ruled out before you began.
- **Supporting context goes last.** If the reader agrees with the recommendation, they may not need this section. Write it for skeptics, not for approval.
- **Topics for live discussion is mandatory.** Pre-reads without it turn meetings into Q&A sessions.
- **Questions that can be answered async** redirect simple clarifications out of meeting time.
- **Document is written to be read silently for 5-10 minutes before discussion begins.**
- **No coaching notes or meta-guidance** embedded in the document. Everything written is for the attendees.
- **Title format:** `[Project/Topic] - Pre-read`, no date in title (date is in the header field).
