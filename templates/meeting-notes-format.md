# Meeting Notes Format

Use this format for all meeting agendas and notes created in the work context.

---

## Template

```
# YYYY-MM-DD [Project] - [Meeting Title]

**Date**
YYYY-MM-DD

## Participants
* Name 1
* Name 2
* Name 3

## Goals
* [Primary goal of the meeting]

## Context (before the meeting)

[Background and relevant context attendees should have before the session.]

## Discussion topics

**1. [Topic title] (X min)**

[Opening sentence framing the topic. Then bullets for questions or sub-points if needed.]
- [Question or sub-point]
- [Question or sub-point]

---

**2. [Topic title] (X min)**

[Opening sentence. Bullets as needed.]
- [Question or sub-point]

## Decisions to leave with

1. [Decision 1]
2. [Decision 2]

## What this session does NOT need to solve

* [Out of scope item]
* [Out of scope item]

## AI Meeting Notes

To add after meeting
```

---

## Rules

- **Title format:** `YYYY-MM-DD [Project] - [Meeting Title]`, date always first
- **Participants:** bullet list of names only
- **Goals:** bullet list, not inline
- **No Duration field**
- **Discussion topics:** numbered bold headings with time estimate, followed by prose and bullets. Separate each topic with a `---` divider.
- **AI Meeting Notes:** always included at the end as a placeholder for post-meeting notes
- **Naming in discussion content:** people who are IN the meeting are never referred to by name, use "you", "the team", or their role instead. People who are NOT in the meeting are referred to by name.
- **Paste-ready content:** the document is written to be shared as-is. Do not embed coaching notes, prep reminders, or meta-guidance for the facilitator inside the discussion content.
